What is the emissions coverage of China’s headline climate targets?

Carbon peaking before 2030
Emissions coverage

CO₂ emissions from fuel combustion, industrial processes and product use 1

Footnote 1: As indicated in The First Biennial Transparency Report on Climate Change of the People’s Republic of China (2024).
Post-peak reduction by 2035: 7–10% below the peak level
Emissions coverage

Total economy-wide net greenhouse gas emissions, encompassing all greenhouse gases 2

Footnote 2: As indicated in China’s 2035 Nationally Determined Contribution Report (2025).
Carbon neutrality before 2060
Emissions coverage

Total economy-wide net greenhouse gas emissions, encompassing all greenhouse gases 3

Footnote 3: The scope of the carbon neutrality target is not explicitly defined in China’s official climate policy documents; however, it was clarified in a speech by Xie Zhenhua, China’s then Special Envoy for Climate Change.
